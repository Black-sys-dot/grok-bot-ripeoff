# Taste

## Workflow

- Verify the change actually works (run it, check the real behavior) before reporting back; do not claim "everything is working fine" without self-verification — and a fix sitting in repo source doesn't count, the build/artifact the user actually runs must reflect it. Confidence: 0.88
- Don't over-fragment work into tiny steps — prefers fewer, larger steps and faster overall progress over slow micro-chunking. Confidence: 0.75
